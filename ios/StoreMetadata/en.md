## Description

Vexi teaches you the flags of the world and then keeps them.

You are shown a flag. You decide whether you knew it — and that is the whole
interaction. What happens next is the part that matters: the app schedules the
ones you found hard to come back soon, and the ones you knew cold to come back
in a week, then a month. A session is ten cards — or five, or twenty, if you
set it so.

Each flag comes with the place behind it: its capital, its population, its
currency and language, and where in the world it actually is, on a satellite
map you can open full screen.

- 250 flags: all 195 countries, plus territories and special areas
- Spaced repetition that adapts to your answers rather than to a timer
- Study by continent, or all 195 countries at once
- Progress you can see: a world map whose continents brighten as you learn them
- Studying works offline, and works without an account
- Free, with no advertising

Sign in if you want your progress to survive a lost phone and follow you to a
new one. Everything else works as a guest. Product analytics and crash reports
stay off until you turn them on.
