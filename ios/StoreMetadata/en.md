## Description

Country Flags teaches you the flags of the world and then keeps them.

You are shown a flag. You decide whether you knew it — and that is the whole
interaction. What happens next is the part that matters: the app schedules the
ones you found hard to come back soon, and the ones you knew cold to come back
in a week, then a month. Studying twenty cards a day is enough to learn the
lot.

Every flag comes with the country behind it: its capital, its population, its
currency and language, and where in the world it actually is, on a real map
you can open full screen.

- 250 flags, drawn crisply at any size
- Spaced repetition that adapts to your answers rather than to a timer
- Study by continent, or take the whole world at once
- Progress you can see: a map that lights up as you learn it
- Works offline, and works without an account
- Free, with no advertising. A few extra decks are an optional one-time purchase.

Sign in if you want your progress to survive a lost phone, or if you want to
buy or restore a deck — a purchase is tied to your account so it follows you
to a new device. Everything else works as a guest, and nothing is collected
until you say so.
