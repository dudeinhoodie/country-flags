## What the app is

Country Flags teaches the flags of the world by spaced repetition: you are shown a flag, you say whether you knew it, and the app decides when to ask again. Most of it is free. Some decks are sold separately as a one-time purchase, described below. There are no subscriptions, and the app shows no advertising.
